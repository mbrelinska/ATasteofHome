Huevos Rancheros Casserole

Grant's mom has been making this breakfast casserole for years, and it didn't take long for Magda to fall in love with it too. Cheesy, comforting, and perfect for a family breakfast.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Eggs5 large
Jack cheese8 oz
Cottage cheese1 cup
Butter, melted1/4 cup
Flour1/4 cup
Baking powder1/2 tsp
Salt1/4 tsp
Diced green chile4 oz

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs.
  2. Add the cottage cheese and melted butter.
  3. Stir in the flour, baking powder, and salt.
  4. Add the Jack cheese and diced green chile.
  5. Mix well and pour into a heavily greased baking pan.
  6. Bake at 350°F for 35 minutes.

Tips & Notes

Feeding a crowd? Double the recipe to serve 8 and bake it in a 9 × 13-inch pan.
