Polish Chicken Soup

For Magda, this soup is a true taste of home. Warm, comforting, and full of vegetables, it has always reminded her of her mom's kitchen. It is perfect for cold days when you need something warm and comforting, and especially when someone is starting to feel under the weather.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Whole chicken or chicken pieces 3 lb
Water 8 cups
Carrots 3
Parsley root 2
Celery root 1 piece
Leek 1
Onion 1
Bay leaves 2
Whole allspice 4
Black peppercorns 6
Salt To taste
Fresh parsley For serving
Thin egg noodles For serving

Instructions

  1. Place the chicken in a large pot and cover it with cold water.
  2. Slowly bring the water to a gentle simmer. Remove any foam that collects on the surface.
  3. Peel the carrots, parsley roots, and celery root. Clean the leek and add all of the vegetables to the pot.
  4. Peel the onion, cut it in half, and lightly brown the cut sides in a dry pan. Add the onion to the soup.
  5. Add the bay leaves, allspice, peppercorns, and salt.
  6. Cook the soup slowly over low heat for about 2 to 3 hours. The broth should simmer gently rather than boil rapidly.
  7. Remove the cooked chicken from the pot. Separate the meat from the bones and shred it into smaller pieces.
  8. Return the shredded chicken to the soup and season with additional salt if needed.
  9. Cook the noodles separately. Serve the soup with noodles, cooked vegetables, chicken, and fresh parsley.

Tips & Notes

The key to a clear, flavorful broth is cooking it slowly over low heat. Let the soup gently simmer and avoid letting it boil too strongly.
