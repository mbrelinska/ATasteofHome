Vegetable Soup

This vegetable soup is one of those simple, comforting meals that tastes like home. Filled with colorful vegetables and a warm, creamy broth, it is especially good on cold days when all you want is a bowl of homemade soup.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Cauliflower 1/2 head
Potatoes 4 medium
Carrots 2
Onion 1
Green beans 1 cup
Green peas 1 cup
Brussels sprouts 1 cup
Vegetable or chicken broth 6 cups
Heavy cream 1/2 cup
Fresh dill 1 small bunch
Salt To taste
Black pepper To taste

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes and carrots. Cut the potatoes into cubes and slice the carrots. Chop the onion.
  2. Cut the cauliflower into small florets. Trim the green beans and Brussels sprouts. Cut larger Brussels sprouts in half.
  3. Pour the broth into a large pot and bring it to a gentle boil.
  4. Add the potatoes, carrots, and onion. Cook until they begin to soften.
  5. Add the cauliflower, green beans, Brussels sprouts, and peas.
  6. Continue cooking over medium heat until all of the vegetables are tender.
  7. Lower the heat and slowly stir in the cream. Do not allow the soup to boil rapidly after adding the cream.
  8. Season with salt and pepper and add chopped fresh dill.
  9. Serve warm with additional dill if desired.

Tips & Notes

Add the vegetables in stages so the softer vegetables do not overcook. The soup should have plenty of vegetables while still leaving enough broth in every bowl.
