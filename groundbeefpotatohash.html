Ground Beef Potato Hash

Magda started making this breakfast when we first moved in together because it was easy to make, perfect for meal prepping, and kept us full for hours. It quickly became one of Grant's favorites. Warm, filling, and loaded with meat and potatoes, it's exactly the kind of breakfast we love in our house.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Vegetable oil2 tbsp
Potatoes, cubed4 cups
Green pepper, diced1
Small onion, finely diced1
Ground beef1 lb
Eggs, beaten6
Salt and pepperTo taste
Crushed red pepper1 tsp

Instructions

  1. Wash and peel the potatoes, then cut them into small cubes. Finely dice the onion and green pepper.
  2. Heat the vegetable oil in a skillet over medium heat.
  3. Add the potatoes, green pepper, and onion. Cook for about 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they begin to soften.
  4. Increase the heat to medium-high and add the ground beef. Cook until the beef is fully cooked and the potatoes begin to brown.
  5. Pour the beaten eggs over the beef and potato mixture. Let them begin to set, then gently mix until the eggs are cooked.
  6. Season with salt, pepper, and crushed red pepper and serve warm.

Tips & Notes

Cut the potatoes into small, evenly sized cubes so they cook at the same rate. This recipe is great for meal prepping and reheating during the week.
