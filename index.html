Welcome to A Taste of Home — recipes that feel like home.

I never really enjoyed cooking when I lived at home with my mom. After moving out, I slowly started to enjoy it and began collecting recipes that became favorites in our home.

My mom has always cooked by feel rather than following written recipes, and over time I realized I started doing the same thing. A Taste of Home is my way of collecting those recipes so they can be shared with family and friends and preserved for the future.

Our Family Tree

Along with collecting family recipes, I have also been working on our family tree. The tree currently reaches back to the early 1800s and late 1700s, and I am still working on it.

Access to the family tree is available by invitation only. Family members who would like access can request an invitation using the form below.
