Pot Roast

This pot roast is the perfect comfort food for a cold day. The beef cooks slowly until tender while the potatoes, carrots, and vegetables soak up the rich sauce. It is warm, filling, and perfect for a cozy dinner at home.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Chuck roast 2½ to 3 lb
Olive oil 1 tbsp
Salt 2 tsp
Black pepper 2 tsp
Paprika 2 tsp
Garlic powder 1 tsp
Onion powder 1 tsp
Oregano 1 tsp
Cayenne pepper ½ tsp
Brown sugar 2 tsp
Yellow onion, diced 1
Garlic, minced 5 cloves
Tomato paste 2 tbsp
Dry red wine 1 cup
Beef broth 2 cups
Bay leaves 2
Fresh thyme As needed
Carrots, chopped 3
Celery stalks, chopped 3
Baby potatoes, washed 1½ lb
Whole garlic cloves 2

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 300°F. Mix the salt, black pepper, paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, cayenne, and brown sugar. Season the roast generously on all sides.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium high heat. Sear the roast on all sides until browned, then remove it from the pot and set it aside.
  3. Add the diced onion to the same pot and cook until softened. Add the minced garlic and cook briefly. Stir in the tomato paste, then add the red wine. Scrape the browned pieces from the bottom of the pot and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Return the roast to the pot. Add the beef broth, bay leaves, and fresh thyme. Cover and bake at 300°F for 2 hours.
  5. Remove the bay leaves and thyme stems. Add the carrots, celery, potatoes, and whole garlic cloves around the roast.
  6. Cover again and bake at 300°F for about another hour, or until the beef is tender enough to easily pull apart and the vegetables are cooked.

Tips & Notes

Let the roast cook slowly until the meat easily pulls apart with a fork. Serve it with the vegetables and plenty of the sauce from the pot.
