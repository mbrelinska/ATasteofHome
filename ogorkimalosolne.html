Polish Lightly Fermented Cucumbers (Ogórki Małosolne)

Ogórki małosolne are traditional Polish lightly fermented cucumbers, especially popular during the summer. They're crunchy, slightly salty, and have a fresh dill flavor. My mom has always made them with just cucumbers, dill, salt, and water. No garlic or other additions needed! They're one of those simple recipes that always remind me of home.

Ingredients

✓ Ingredient Amount
Small pickling cucumbers 2.2 lb
Water About 8 1/2 cups
Non-iodized pickling salt About 2 heaping tbsp*
Fresh dill with stems and flower heads 5 sprigs

*For a consistent brine, use approximately 1 tablespoon of fine pickling salt per quart of water. Salt amounts vary by grain size.

Instructions

  1. Wash the cucumbers thoroughly. Soak them in cold water for about 1 hour to help keep them crisp.
  2. Wash the dill and prepare a large, clean glass jar.
  3. Place some dill at the bottom of the jar.
  4. Pack the cucumbers tightly into the jar, adding the remaining dill between them.
  5. Prepare the brine by dissolving the salt in water. Use enough brine to completely cover the cucumbers.
  6. Pour the brine into the jar, making sure all the cucumbers stay submerged. Use a clean fermentation weight if needed.
  7. Cover the jar loosely with a lid so fermentation gases can escape. Place it on a plate and leave at room temperature, away from direct sunlight.
  8. After about 2 days, start tasting the cucumbers. They usually develop their lightly fermented flavor within 2–4 days, depending on room temperature.
  9. Once they taste the way you like, refrigerate them to slow down fermentation.

Tips & Notes

Choose small, firm pickling cucumbers rather than regular slicing cucumbers. Keep them completely submerged in the brine throughout fermentation.

The longer they ferment, the more sour they become. Refrigerate when they reach your preferred flavor. Discard the batch if you notice fuzzy mold or a rotten smell.
